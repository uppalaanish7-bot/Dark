TAKE THE ARENA WITH YOU

One more wave.
Anywhere.

Install RELIC on Android, or add the web game to your home screen on Android and iPhone.

Get Android APK ↓

The web app saves the game for offline play after its download completes.

Android

Download the APK, open it on your phone, and follow Android’s install prompts. Requires Android 7 or newer on a 64-bit ARM device with OpenGL ES 3 support.

iPhone & iPad

Open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Launch RELIC and turn your phone sideways.

PC

Open the browser game in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari with WebGL 2. Click Enter the Arena, then Deploy.

Native iOS App Store installation is not included.
