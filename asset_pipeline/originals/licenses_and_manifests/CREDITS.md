# HERO credits

## Supplied characters, guardians and sword

The current game uses the supplied Girl Knight and Omega Knight, Da Vinci sword, nine sub-bosses and their soldiers, the grand guardian and guard, and the main boss. Individual original authors, model titles, source URLs and licenses are recorded in [assets/provided/ATTRIBUTION.md](assets/provided/ATTRIBUTION.md). The [source manifest](assets/provided/source_manifest.json) identifies each original and runtime derivative.

These models remain the work of their original creators. Runtime modifications include polygon reduction, texture resizing, coordinate normalization, grounding, sword attachment, material adjustments, retargeted skeletal motion and procedural animation. Eighteen sculpt derivatives now use fitted 17-bone rigs, including the grand guardian's wings. Girl Knight and Stelae have repaired binding/grounding derivatives; the soldier's sword and Praetor's rifle retain the supplied geometry with corrected runtime hand attachments. Girl Knight replaces the earlier damaged Hornet file; the damaged file is not used as the player.

## Project-created work

The mobile action icons under `assets/ui/controls/` are original project artwork. Their circular thumb-control layout follows familiar mobile action-game conventions; no Free Fire artwork is included. Girl Knight's repaired runtime rig has a three-segment cape, grounded feet, continuous idle blends and 51 motion clips. The current arena lighting/material pass was developed in coordination with Antigravity.

The Cathedral of Cinders architecture, eleven-floor layout, connecting staircases, terrace and healing potion, floor and portal shaders, lighting, combat effects, interface, game code and synthesized audio were created for this project. The sword is the supplied Da Vinci model credited in the attribution document.

The Hollow Spire exterior is original Blender geometry and procedurally baked basalt colour, normal and roughness textures generated by `tools/create_hollow_spire.py`. Its editable source, geometry report and previews are under `art/hollow_spire/`; its runtime mesh is `assets/buildings/hollow_spire.glb`.

## Animation source

Authored humanoid motion from **Quaternius's Universal Animation Library**, dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**, is retargeted to the supplied knight rigs. The game adds rig calibration, blends, movement overlays, foot-height stabilization and cloth follow-through. Project-authored player choreography includes the upright guard, distinct sword cuts, overhead mana gathering and downstroke, and aimed sword-tip casting. Enemy procedural motion and weapon-specific action poses are project-created derivatives of the supplied models. The original animation notice is preserved in `License.txt` and included as `ANIMATION-LICENSE.txt` in the Windows build. [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/) · [CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
