## Changes made for this game

October 10 animation update: derivatives under `assets/sculpt_motion/` retain source UVs and materials and add fitted 17-bone rigs to eighteen enemy models, with corrected facing, elbows, knees, ankles and separate cloth/equipment weights. Source GLBs are unchanged. These use procedural locomotion and combat poses. Girl Knight and Stelae also receive binding/grounding repair derivatives generated by `tools/repair_character_bindings.gd`. Stelae Sentinel receives a retargeted Quaternius CC0 animation library. See `art/asset_review/sculpt_rigs.json` and `tools/build_sculpt_rigs.gd` for the sculpt inventory and reproducible process. Runtime source sword/rifle attachments are repaired by `scripts/enemy_loadout.gd`.

Enemy derivatives are grounded and scaled to a consistent 2.1 m baseline. Expensive sculpt meshes are reduced to approximately 28,000 triangles for soldiers and 65,000 triangles for bosses (UV seams can increase the final count). Source rigs are preserved; high-resolution maps are reduced to 1K for soldiers and 2K for bosses. Godot can generate additional mesh LODs.

Girl Knight uses the original supplied 3,596-triangle mesh and palette. An 18-bone deformation rig was added. Omega Knight retains the supplied 167-bone rig. Both use 35 authored retargeted clips from the Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0), with A-pose calibration, blended locomotion, sword-combo variations, casting, jump/roll poses, moving attack overlays and secondary cape motion. Both player models are grounded at 1.82 m. The supplied Da Vinci sword follows the animated hand, with indoor material adaptation and living fire VFX; there is no gun.

The supplied rigs of subboss_1, subboss_7 and soldier_1 also use retargeted skeletal motion. Eighteen other enemy variants use the additional region-weighted rigs described above, with articulated body, limb, wing and robe motion layered over eleven keyed elemental attack profiles. Heavy figures stay grounded; spectral casters hover. Rejected automatic heat-weight experiments remain excluded.

The damaged Hornet download is excluded. The manifest records source, runtime and animation-library hashes for repeatability.
Source inventory and exact changes: [source_manifest.json](source_manifest.json)

The preserved grand-boss GLB has a three-bone feather hinge rig authored in Blender. Its active `sculpt_motion` derivative extends articulation to seventeen regions, including shoulders, elbows, torso, legs, ankles and wings, with wing extension following the levitation windup and diving strike.
